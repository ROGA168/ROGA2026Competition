为什么参加本次赛事？

Why Join This Tournament?

Sishu Graha 议会制辩论赛是印度规模较大的中学生辩论赛事之一，本届为第六届。赛事适合已掌握 WSDC 基础规则、准备进入完整赛事场景的学生。核心裁判团包含与印度国家辩论队相关的教练与选拔官，可保障辩题质量，并提供围绕青年辩手成长的详尽反馈，尤其适合正在向完整时长竞技发言转型的学生。

Sishu Graha Parliamentary Debate is one of India’s largest school debating competitions, now in its 6th edition. It is suitable for students who already understand WSDC fundamentals and are ready for a full tournament setting. The adjudication core includes coaches and selectors linked to the Indian national debate team, ensuring high-quality motions and detailed feedback.
